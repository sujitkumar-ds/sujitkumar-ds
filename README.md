## About Me

I am a Statistics graduate interested in statistical analysis, data science, and artificial intelligence, with applications in public health and computational biology. My research interests include statistical modeling, machine learning, health data analysis, and bioinformatics.

I am also interested in developing practical research software, Python and R tools, and reproducible analytical workflows that support data-driven research.

- **Research:** Statistical analysis, modeling, machine learning, and AI
- **Applications:** Public health, healthcare analytics, and bioinformatics
- **Development:** Data Science tools, and Python/R packages
- **Contact:** [sujit.kumar.stat@gmail.com](mailto:sujit.kumar.stat@gmail.com)
